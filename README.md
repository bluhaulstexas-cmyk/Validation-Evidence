# Synthetic validation evidence: experiments 06 and 07

This repository contains public research materials for two synthetic case studies: structured arithmetic validation and a separate deterministic evidence gate. It is an experimental record, not a product release or evidence of general AI reliability.

## Current naming
The related flagship project is now **Binder**, with the tagline **“A calmer mailbox.”** Earlier project names include CommonGround and Decision Desk; “Binder AI” is retired. **Binder Business** is the trades application, **Binder Personal** is a future addition, and the sub-brand endorsement is **“powered by Binder.”** Date Night remains a separate side project.

[Tim Hauptrief](https://timhauptrief.com/) remains the personal brand behind the work. Historical experiment files retain their original names, dates, methods, and limitations. This naming update does not change any experimental result or establish product readiness.

## Materials
- `exp06-prereg.md` and `exp07-prereg.md`: recorded plans and expected responses; timing has not been independently certified.
- `exp06-results.json` and `exp07-results.json`: reported inputs and outcomes.
- `fixture-*.md` and `FIXTURES.md`: invented test data, not real financial information.
- `agent-a-independent-review-exp07.md`: a sanitized summary of a documented review, not academic peer review.

## Findings and limits
A proposal passed arithmetic validation against an intentionally wrong configured budget. A separate gate held conflicting structured values and treated agreement under distinct declared lineages as unverified. The checks were separate, not an integrated pipeline.

Six of six and ten of ten authored predictions matched the recorded outcomes. These are specification-conformance counts, not estimates of accuracy on unseen inputs. The tests were author-designed and non-blind. No authenticated source independence, general natural-language conflict detection, or model-level injection resistance was demonstrated.

## Reproducibility and redaction
Inputs, predictions, and reported outputs can be inspected. Implementation code is not released, so the original execution cannot be fully reproduced from this package. Internal document identifiers are replaced with stable synthetic labels. Scientific values, outcomes, timestamps, and reported limitations are preserved. The review is an editorial summary, not an unchanged original artifact. Product plans and future experiment proposals are outside this package's scope.
